## Essential Calculators & Clinical Tools

*   **MELD-Na Score:** [MDCalc MELD-Na](https://www.mdcalc.com/meld-na-score-model-end-stage-liver-disease-12-older) — For transplant prioritization and cirrhosis mortality risk.
*   **Child-Pugh Classification:** [MDCalc Child-Pugh](https://www.mdcalc.com/child-pugh-score-cirrhosis-mortality) — Core assessment tool for hepatic functional reserve.
*   **FIB-4 Index:** [MDCalc FIB-4](https://www.mdcalc.com/fibrosis-4-fib-4-index-liver-fibrosis) — Non-invasive screening for hepatic fibrosis.
*   **Lille Model:** [Lille Model Calculator](https://www.lillemodel.com) — Assessing 6-month survival in acute alcoholic hepatitis post-corticosteroids.
